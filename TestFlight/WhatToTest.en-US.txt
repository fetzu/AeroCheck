6.8.0 draws its own map, the Atlas, from OpenStreetMap, on every map of the app.

What to try:
- Settings › Offline data replaces Data & Storage and Navigation & Maps: one status at the top, your countries, the worldwide airports and the Atlas overview.
- Offline data › Add a country: a country downloads at one press, its Atlas with its airspace, aerodromes and VFR procedures (Switzerland is about 90 MB, Wi-Fi recommended). Then turn on airplane mode: the map still draws there.
- A large country (France, Germany) opens its page: all of it at one press, or the regions you fly. Download a neighbour too: at the border both sides draw, with no gap and nothing twice.
- Map sheet › Base chart: Atlas and Aerial imagery replace Apple's two layers. On Aerial imagery, pan from Switzerland into France or Germany: each source is credited at the foot of the map.
- Zoom out to Europe: every country drawn in detail, with its cities, roads and relief.
- Plan › Map, the Cockpit's MAP and the route editor: tap an aerodrome, a circuit's altitude, a navaid; drag a route point; the legs band; Search aerodromes. Tell us anything that moves differently from 6.7.
- A flight in the logbook: its track map, and the share card on Atlas and on Aerial imagery.
- Plan a flight crossing a border: the banner offers the Atlas regions along the route.
- The Swiss ICAO, glider and national charts: past their edge, the Atlas now shows under them.
