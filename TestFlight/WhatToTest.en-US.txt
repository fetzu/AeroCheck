6.5.0 speaks German, and prepares a flight for the countries it goes to: the briefing links, the weather and closing the flight plan follow each country's own rules.

What to try:
- German: set the device to German (Settings › General › Language & Region), or the app alone (Settings › AéroCheck › Language). Every screen, the Cockpit's buttons included: say what reads wrong.
- A flight's page, Prepare: the Weather block above the tasks, with the METAR and TAF of each field (the nearest TAF for a field without one), the SIGMETs along the route, and each country's weather and area-forecast pages as rows. One tick for all of it; ticked, it folds to one line. The DABS says which day's it opens, or when the flight's comes out.
- A route abroad, Bressaucourt to Freiburg for one: Germany's links, and after landing the close-out card with Germany's rule and number. A landing in Switzerland keeps skyguide's number. On a trip, each leg counts the trip's preparation.
- A departure from a Swedish field without a tower: the line about activating the plan yourself. A route through Belarus: the red row, and START FLIGHT held a second.
- The maps share one chart; outside Switzerland they show Apple Maps in place of the ICAO chart. The OpenAIP layer draws again (OpenAIP moved its tiles), and Settings › Data & Storage downloads faster: airspace now comes from OpenAIP's own files.
- In the US, near Palo Alto (KPAO) or San Carlos (KSQL): the pattern side at each runway end and the TPA on the approach view. Official charts for the US, Australia and New Zealand.
