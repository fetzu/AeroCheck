FONTLOG for the B612 fonts served by aerocheck.app

The five files are subsets (217 glyphs) of B612 1.008 (https://github.com/polarsys/b612, tag 1.008)
and are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt, next to this file).

b612-regular.woff2, b612-bold.woff2, b612-italic.woff2
  Unmodified subsets.

b612-mono-regular.woff2, b612-mono-bold.woff2
  Modified (2026-10-01), as the app's B612 Mono is (AeroCheck PR #246). B612 Mono draws the colon,
  semicolon, period, comma, apostrophe and middle dot at the left of their monospaced cell (upstream
  issue #31), so numbers read "00: 44" and "122. 050". Those glyphs, and the right single quote (a few
  units off), were moved sideways until their ink is centred in the cell. Nothing else changed: every
  advance is still 1300 units, the outlines keep their shape, the character map and the family and
  PostScript names are the original ones (the licence declares no Reserved Font Name). The name table
  records the change in the version string ("Version 1.008; AeroCheck: punctuation centred"), the
  unique id and the description.

  To reproduce, run scripts/center-b612-mono-punctuation.py (fontTools with brotli) on the subsets:

    before  b612-mono-bold.woff2     sha256 76dc8f1668f8ccfd2d3a4360133ec65c51644914b56f8e1b20527b4a24b75a38
    after   b612-mono-regular.woff2  sha256 bf1b8b344cc4a6d21851c963c70103f6ddae0efffe8ca45e68a02acd983e49d3
