6.1.2 is the version going to the App Store, in place of 6.1.1. Use it as you normally would, before, during and after your flights, and send what you notice from the TestFlight app: a screenshot with a line of text is the quickest way.

Fixed since 6.1.1: on iOS 27, AéroCheck could crash at launch with iCloud signed in, every time after a fresh install. If you are on iOS 27, delete the app, install this build and open it: it should start, and your flights should come back from iCloud.

New since 6.1.0: an iPad without GPS (Wi-Fi only) can install AéroCheck. In flight it uses the iPhone's GPS through Companion mode (iOS 26 on both), or an external GPS receiver. If you fly with either, tell us whether the GPS indicator stays green.
