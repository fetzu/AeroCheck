6.8.0 draws its own map, the Pilot's map, from OpenStreetMap, on every map of the app.

What to try:
- Settings › Data & Storage › Pilot's map: download Switzerland (about 90 MB, Wi-Fi recommended). Then turn on airplane mode: the map still draws in Switzerland.
- Map sheet › Base chart: Pilot's map and Aerial imagery replace Apple's two layers. On Aerial imagery, pan from Switzerland into France or Germany: each source is credited at the foot of the map.
- Zoom out to Europe: the world outline, with the countries' names inside them and their capitals.
- Plan › Map, the Cockpit's MAP and the route editor: tap an aerodrome, a circuit's altitude, a navaid; drag a route point; the legs band; Search aerodromes. Tell us anything that moves differently from 6.7.
- A flight in the logbook: its track map, and the share card on Pilot's map and on Aerial imagery.
- Plan a flight crossing a border: the banner offers the pilot's map regions along the route.
- The Swiss ICAO, glider and national charts: past their edge, the pilot's map now shows under them.
