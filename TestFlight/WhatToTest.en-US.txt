6.4.3 lets you tell the app when you change frequency: when it is time to call, NEXT comes due, and a tap on it makes it NOW. Without a tap the app changes by itself at the latest point. Either way UNDO takes it back.

What to try, on a route (a dry run on the ground does it too: Plan › Routes, swipe right on a route):
- at the level-off NEXT gets an outline, and a line along its foot that runs out; NOW stays on the departure;
- tap NEXT when you change frequency: it becomes NOW, and the slot reads "… now" with UNDO for 6 seconds;
- don't tap: at the latest point (out of the departure's zone or 10 NM out, a zone's or an Info's boundary, 30 seconds before a reporting or arrival point) the app changes by itself, "… now, automatically", with UNDO; undone, the call stays due and the app doesn't change again;
- a LISTEN in NEXT (the destination's ATIS): a tap marks it heard, and NEXT goes on to the next call;
- ROUTE › RADIO: a tap on a row makes it NOW, as when a controller sends you to another station;
- on the iPhone the due call shows on the NOW line, and a tap on the line switches; on a Companion iPhone the tap switches the iPad, and UNDO there takes it back;
- the guided dry run (Settings › About › Fly a dry run on the ground) has a card for it after the cruise check.

On the iPad the Cockpit's blocks are now evenly spaced, 10 pt apart from the header down.
