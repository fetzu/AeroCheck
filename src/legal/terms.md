## 1.0 Safety notice

### 1.1 AéroCheck is supplemental information only

AéroCheck is **not certified avionics**. It is not approved by EASA, the FOCA, the FAA or any other aviation authority, it has not been developed to any aviation software assurance standard, and it must not be relied upon as a primary source of information for any phase of flight.

Treat it the way you would treat a well-meaning passenger with a tablet: possibly helpful, never authoritative.

### 1.2 The aircraft's own documents are authoritative

The checklists in AéroCheck are **transcriptions** of documents belonging to flying clubs and aircraft operators. They are not approved checklists, they are not maintained by the aircraft manufacturer, and they may be incomplete, out of date, or simply wrong.

The Aircraft Flight Manual (AFM), the Pilot's Operating Handbook (POH), any applicable supplements or STCs, and the operator's own approved checklist remain the official documentation for the aircraft. Where AéroCheck and those documents disagree, **those documents are correct and AéroCheck is not**.

Compare any checklist in this app against the aircraft's approved checklist before using it in flight. The reference speeds, stall speeds, crosswind limits and configuration figures shown in the app come from the same transcriptions and carry the same caveat.

### 1.3 Not a navigation system

The moving map, airspace, terrain, obstacles, navaids, reporting points, airport data, frequencies and flight-planning features are for situational awareness only. AéroCheck must **not** be used as a primary means of navigation, and must not be used for IFR navigation at all.

Aeronautical data comes from third parties (swisstopo, OpenAIP, OurAirports, Natural Earth, and others credited in the app). It is not guaranteed to be current, complete or accurate, and it is not a substitute for the official AIP, NOTAM, or a proper pre-flight briefing. Airspace structures, frequencies and obstacles change; the copy on your device may be months old.

### 1.4 Weather is an observation, not a forecast for your flight

METAR, TAF, SIGMET and wind data are relayed from third-party providers as-is. They may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable, and the app may fail to fetch them without saying so. Never launch on the strength of what this app showed you: obtain an official meteorological briefing.

### 1.5 GPS, altitude and speed are approximations

Positions, ground speed, estimated airspeed, altitude and barometric readings are derived from consumer-grade device sensors. They are not a certified altimeter, airspeed indicator or position source. The device may lose its fix, drift, or continue to display a stale value.

Automatic detection of takeoffs, landings, go-arounds and touch-and-goes is a convenience for your logbook. It gets things wrong. Review your flight log before relying on it for currency, licensing or billing purposes.

### 1.6 It is a tablet, and tablets fail

Battery exhaustion, overheating in direct sun, an OS update, a crash, or simply dropping the thing behind the seat will all take AéroCheck out of the cockpit at once. Carry the paper checklist and the charts you would carry if the app did not exist.

### 1.7 You are the pilot in command

Nothing in AéroCheck relieves you of your responsibilities as pilot in command or operator, or of your obligation to comply with all applicable regulations and operating rules.

**You are solely responsible for the safe conduct of every flight**, for every decision you make before and during it, for verifying every item of information this app shows you against official and certified sources, and for exercising your own judgement. Do not let the app fly the aeroplane, and do not let it think for you.
