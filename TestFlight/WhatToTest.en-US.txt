6.4.2 makes the radio's NOW and NEXT follow your route the way you work the radio: the stations in the order you call them, as on the printed nav log. And the app now draws the maps itself, without holes.

What to look at, on a route:
- the departure field stays NOW until you level off (or leave its zone), with the Info or the zone ahead as NEXT;
- then each Info and each zone in turn: a CTR, RMZ or FIZ becomes NOW 5 minutes before you enter it, the next Info shows as NEXT before its boundary and becomes NOW there;
- a TMA cleared by the Info (Geneva, Zurich, the Basel sectors) is named beside NOW from 10 minutes before;
- over the Ajoie and the northern Jura, from 2,000 ft above the ground: Bale Info;
- the destination becomes NOW 5 minutes before the aerodrome, or 2 minutes before its arrival point if that comes first, and its ATIS shows in NEXT as LISTEN from 10 minutes before;
- an aerodrome the route passes within 2 NM shows in NEXT as LISTEN from 5 minutes before, NOW staying with the Info (Settings › Checklist & Flight › Aerodromes on the way turns it off).

Without a route, an aerodrome is NOW only inside its zone or within 3 NM, else the Info.

On the maps (Plan › Map, the Cockpit's MAP, the route editor):
- every chart tile drawn: while the sharp tiles come, a coarser chart stands in, never a hole or Apple's map through the chart;
- the ICAO chart down to the glider chart, drawn larger at its closest zoom;
- the route editor's ICAO/Glider/National map down to the streets, the downloaded charts offline, Apple's map past the border;
- no white fade round the field any more when you zoom in on an aerodrome.
