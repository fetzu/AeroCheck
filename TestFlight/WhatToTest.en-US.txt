6.9.0 puts each airspace's floor and ceiling on the map, and tells you which Swiss areas are active today.

What to try:
- Start a flight and watch the Cockpit's map: tags name the airspace that matters now. Menu › Label every airspace (the EVERY AIRSPACE chip) labels all of them.
- Plan › Map: the limits along the boundaries (Map sheet › Floors & ceilings) and the ground height.
- Tap the map anywhere: the stack of airspaces over that point.
- Fly or replay a route towards controlled airspace: AIRSPACE AHEAD appears in the status slot.
- In Switzerland, an R or D area that is off today is faded, and doesn't trigger AIRSPACE AHEAD.
- Tap an aerodrome: the card in fixed rows, on the ground and in flight.
- Plan a flight across a border: the flight page tells you if you need a flight plan. After take-off from an aerodrome without a tower, REPORT DEPARTURE reminds you. Circuits ask for neither.
- Plan a route through a country without downloaded airspace: the flight page says so and offers it.
- On a fresh install, the first flight you plan shows one page about permissions (Settings › iCloud & Log › Permissions afterwards).
- Hide a club in Settings, then pick one of its aircraft under Premium aircraft: it stays selected.

Send what you notice from the TestFlight app: a screenshot with a line of text is the quickest way.
