6.2.0 takes the Cockpit apart: three pages (CHECKLIST, MAP, ROUTE) between a band at the top that reads the flight and four fixed buttons at the foot. The map can also draw traffic circuits and VFR routes from open flightmaps. Try it on the ground first: plan a flight with a route, START FLIGHT, then walk through the phases.

What to look at:
- the bottom row: the same four places on every page, CHECK is now the second button;
- ROUTE: the destination line, the legs (a tap shows the leg on MAP) and every frequency in the order you'll need it;
- MAP: the chart alone, the buttons on the right edge, and the slot at the top left (UNDO after MARK, CHART OFFLINE in airplane mode, BRIEFING before departure and in descent);
- the iPhone: the phase bar now sits inside the phase button (a tap picks a phase), and the iPhone on its side;
- Plan › Map › Map: Traffic circuits and Arrival & departure routes (with sectors), once the data is downloaded in Settings › Navigation & Maps;
- Companion mode (iOS 26 on both): Divert from the iPhone.
