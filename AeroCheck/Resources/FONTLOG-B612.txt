FONTLOG for the B612 fonts bundled with AeroCheck

The four files come from B612 1.008 (https://github.com/polarsys/b612, tag 1.008, commit 48ac6ba)
and are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-B612.txt, next to this file). All four are
modified; the family and PostScript names are the original ones (the licence declares no Reserved
Font Name), and each change is recorded in the name table: version string, unique id, description.

B612Mono-Regular.ttf, B612Mono-Bold.ttf: punctuation centred (2026-10-01)
  B612 Mono draws the colon, semicolon, period, comma, apostrophe and middle dot at the left of
  their monospaced cell (upstream issue #31), so numbers read "00: 44", "122. 050" and, with the
  Swiss grouping apostrophe, "1' 860". Those glyphs, and the right single quote (a few units off),
  were moved sideways until their ink is centred in the cell. Nothing else changed: every advance is
  still 1300 units and the outlines keep their shape and hinting. Version string: "Version 1.008;
  AeroCheck: punctuation centred". Made by scripts/center-b612-mono-punctuation.py.

All four files: the copyright and registered signs at full size (2026-10-06)
  B612 draws © and ® as superscripts in every face: a circle 940 units across (47 % of the em) hung
  from the cap height, its foot 577 units above the baseline, so "Chart © swisstopo / BAZL" read
  like a footnote mark. Both were scaled uniformly (same shape, the ring and the letter in
  proportion) until as tall as the capital O, from the baseline to the cap height with the O's
  overshoot (-17 to 1517 units of 2000), and given the O's side bearings, evened out: their advance
  grows from 1100 to 1782 units in B612 Regular and 1698 in B612 Bold. B612 Mono keeps its 1300-unit
  cell, so there they are as large as the cell allows with the O's side bearings (1100 units across
  in Regular, 1198 in Bold) and centred in the cell and on the cap height. Upstream gives the two
  glyphs no hinting; the hdmx and LTSH entries follow the new advance. Nothing else changed. Version
  string: "...; AeroCheck: full-size copyright and registered signs". Made by
  scripts/b612-full-size-copyright.py.

To reproduce, run the two scripts (fontTools) on the upstream files, in this order:

  1. scripts/center-b612-mono-punctuation.py on B612Mono-Regular.ttf and B612Mono-Bold.ttf;
  2. scripts/b612-full-size-copyright.py on all four (it refuses an upstream B612 Mono: step 1 first).
