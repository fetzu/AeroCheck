6.5.0 speaks German, and prepares a flight for the countries it goes to: the briefing links, the weather and closing the flight plan follow each country's own rules.

What to try:
- German: set the device to German (Settings › General › Language & Region), or the app alone (Settings › AéroCheck › Language). Every screen, the Cockpit's buttons included: say what reads wrong.
- A flight's page, Prepare: the weather card above the tasks (METAR and TAF at each field, the nearest TAF for a field without one, the SIGMETs along the route); one weather and one NOTAM task with a link per country; airspace activity and the area forecast per country; filing in the departure's country. Under a link, whether it needs a free account or costs money.
- A route abroad, Bressaucourt to Freiburg for one: Germany's links, and after landing the close-out card with Germany's rule and number. A landing in Switzerland keeps skyguide's number.
- A departure from a Swedish field without a tower: the line on ATC flight plan filed about activating it yourself. A route through Belarus: the red row.
- In the US, the approach view near Palo Alto (KPAO) or San Carlos (KSQL): the pattern side at each runway end and the TPA; the approach briefing has the FAA's remark. Official charts for the US, Australia and New Zealand.
