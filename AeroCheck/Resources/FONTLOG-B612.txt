FONTLOG for the B612 fonts bundled with AeroCheck

The four files come from B612 1.008 (https://github.com/polarsys/b612, tag 1.008, commit 48ac6ba)
and are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-B612.txt, next to this file).

B612-Regular.ttf, B612-Bold.ttf
  Unmodified.

B612Mono-Regular.ttf, B612Mono-Bold.ttf
  Modified (2026-10-01). B612 Mono draws the colon, semicolon, period, comma, apostrophe and middle
  dot at the left of their monospaced cell (upstream issue #31), so numbers read "00: 44",
  "122. 050" and, with the Swiss grouping apostrophe, "1' 860". Those glyphs, and the right single
  quote (a few units off), were moved sideways until their ink is centred in the cell. Nothing else
  changed: every advance is still 1300 units, the outlines keep their shape and hinting, and the
  family and PostScript names are the original ones (the licence declares no Reserved Font Name).
  The name table records the change in the version string ("Version 1.008; AeroCheck: punctuation
  centred"), the unique id and the description.

  To reproduce, run scripts/center-b612-mono-punctuation.py (fontTools) on the upstream files:

    upstream B612Mono-Regular.ttf  sha256 b98cb96cc8a6206dae08c063d60902df7e6d40f86139ebdb97256704253c9c69
    upstream B612Mono-Bold.ttf     sha256 b467b1d19fdabed42be51d87e38c86645ceeff2f828f294775188d00d1fd68ca
    patched  B612Mono-Regular.ttf  sha256 88a2d8a05caa80d857fd7c2807603e3921d53b3004cc88ba60fe28c714215604
    patched  B612Mono-Bold.ttf     sha256 b4397515fc88b3a970f7609d42f0638b572d0c68fd277546114b846989adc2f3

  (The patched hashes were made with fontTools 4.66.1; another version may lay the tables out
  differently while drawing the same glyphs.)
