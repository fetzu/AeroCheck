6.3.1 fixes one thing in 6.3.0: a landing logged twice, seconds apart (as older flights can carry), showed as a stop-and-go in the flight's TIMELINE, in the logbook line's remarks and on the share card. Full stops less than a minute apart now count as one landing.

What to look at:
- the Logbook: a flight's TIMELINE lists each touch-and-go, go-around and stop-and-go with its time, and no stop-and-go a few seconds before the Landing;
- everything 6.3.0 brought is still worth a try on the ground: a circuits session from Today › CIRCUITS (a full stop, After Landing, then NEW CIRCUIT or PARKING CHECK), and Aircraft › “Send us an aircraft’s checklist” (a request is real and reaches AéroCheck, so send one only for an aircraft you want in the app).
