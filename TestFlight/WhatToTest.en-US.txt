6.10.0 brings NOTAMs into the app: on the flight page, the aerodrome card and the map, for 36 countries.

What to try:
- Plan a flight across a border and open its page: under the official pages, "Also found along your flight" lists the NOTAMs on your route, departure first. Tap a row, or All at the foot of the box.
- In a NOTAM, switch between Original and Spelled out: the app keeps your choice. Full NOTAM shows the whole text.
- Tap an aerodrome that has NOTAMs: its card has a NOTAM row, in flight too.
- Plan › Map, Map sheet: turn on NOTAM areas. Over a country without a list, the chip says NOTAMS NOT SHOWN HERE.
- Outside Switzerland, a NOTAM that activates an area (a parachuting zone, a temporary restricted area) gets a tag and AIRSPACE AHEAD, like an R area.
- Malta is in Settings › Offline data.
- In the Cockpit's MAP, during a flight or a dry run: the map follows the aircraft smoothly at every zoom. Pan away, then tap the arrow at the edge or Centre. Flick the map and stop it with a finger: it stays where you stopped it.
- A long flight should use less battery than with 6.9.0.
- If your logbook never reached your other devices through iCloud, it should now: the first launch sends it once.
