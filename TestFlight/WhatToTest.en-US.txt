6.6.0 rebuilds the flight's page in blocks, and lets a trip change on the way.

What to try:
- Plan a flight for tomorrow and open its page: the line under the title says when it leaves and what's left, and the weather and NOTAMs wait for the day. One tick per block. Set the flight to today: the weather and NOTAMs open.
- On the iPad, Prepare in two columns; on the iPhone, each block in a sheet of its own.
- The Weather block: the METAR, the TAF and the SIGMETs in words, RAW for the report as sent. Say what reads wrong in your language.
- The flight plan block says by when to file: 60 minutes before off-block from Switzerland, each country's own rule elsewhere.
- The pencil next to the title: Flight details, Edit route, Name this flight. On a trip's leg: Leg details, Name this leg, Name this trip.
- A trip with a stop on the way: in flight, Divert lists the next stops with Fly on. After a landing at the next leg's field, the page asks whether you skipped the stop. Leg 2 asks for the weather again, even with leg 1's ticked. Cancel whole trip works from any leg.
- A saved route (Plan › Routes): open an aerodrome between its ends, then When this route is flown › Land here. Plan new flight from that route starts with the stop on.
- Every sheet: Close or Cancel on the left, Done on the right. Tell us about one that doesn't.
- Today and Plan › Flights say what the flight's page says.

Send what you notice from the TestFlight app: a screenshot with a line of text is the quickest way.
