6.1.4 is the version going to the App Store, in place of 6.1.1 to 6.1.3. Use it as you normally would, before, during and after your flights, and send what you notice from the TestFlight app: a screenshot with a line of text is the quickest way.

Fixed since 6.1.1: on a fresh install with iCloud signed in, AéroCheck crashed at launch on iOS 27, then iCloud sync restarted itself over and over without bringing your flights back. To try it, delete the app, install this build and open it: your flights should come back from iCloud.

New in 6.1.4: a subscription never runs out in flight. A flight started with AéroCheck Pro keeps its checklist until you end it, even if the subscription ends on the way. And a new install that never subscribed no longer says "Your subscription has lapsed" in Settings.

New since 6.1.0: an iPad without GPS (Wi-Fi only) can install AéroCheck. In flight it uses the iPhone's GPS through Companion mode (iOS 26 on both), or an external GPS receiver. If you fly with either, tell us whether the GPS indicator stays green.
