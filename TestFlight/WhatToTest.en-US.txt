6.7.0 brings your own notes on each aerodrome, and one map drawing everywhere.

What to try:
- Plan › Map, tap an aerodrome: its card has the frequencies, Chart and Note. Note opens your note in two parts, Arrival and On the ground. Write one, then tap the aerodrome again.
- Search aerodromes finds a field by what your note says, and shows the line that matched. Everything is listed nearest first.
- A flight's Route block has a line per aerodrome with your note. After landing, CLOSE asks "Anything to remember about …?".
- In flight the note reads only, Arrival part only: in the card, the approach briefing and Divert.
- Settings › iCloud & Log › Aerodrome notes: every note, Share all notes (text file), delete one or all. With Sync to iCloud on, the notes follow you to your other devices.
- The maps: past the Swiss charts' edge, Apple's map shows instead of black. Zoom in on Plan › Map or the Cockpit's MAP: the ICAO chart, then the glider chart, then the National Map.
- The route builder's card: + Route adds the aerodrome to the route.

Send what you notice from the TestFlight app: a screenshot with a line of text is the quickest way.
