6.4.1 fixes how landings are counted and what the radio's NEXT suggests on the way in, all found by replaying a real flight on the ground.

What to look at:
- a flight to an aerodrome higher than the one you left, on an iPhone or a cellular iPad (they have a barometer): the landing is counted when it happens, the after landing check comes by itself and the destination gets its time;
- FULL STOP LANDING pressed late, minutes after the touchdown: the flight's review and the logbook show one landing, at the touchdown, and the flight time is the measured one;
- the Logbook: a flight that recorded the same landing twice shows one landing everywhere, its line, its page and the CSV and PDF exports;
- the radio on the way in: once a waypoint outside the destination's circuit is behind you, NEXT stays empty rather than suggesting the FIS, and stays so after landing. On a local flight, NEXT offers the FIS on the way out, until the first point outside the circuit.

Send what you notice from the TestFlight app: a screenshot with a line of text is the quickest way.
